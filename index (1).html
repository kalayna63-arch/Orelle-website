Shipping Policy

A little journey from us to you 🤍

Every Orelle order is carefully prepared, wrapped, and sent on its way with love. We want the experience to feel special from the moment you place your order to the moment it reaches your hands.

✦ Processing Your Order

Your order will usually be packed and ready to leave us within 1–3 business days

During new launches, festive seasons, or special collections, we may need a little extra time to prepare your order. We promise it will be worth the wait.

✦ Delivery

Once your order has been dispatched, delivery generally takes 3–7 business days, depending on your location.

You'll receive tracking details once your parcel begins its journey, so you can keep an eye on it along the way.

✦ Shipping Charges

We're finalising the complete shipping details, including delivery timelines and the areas we currently serve.

In the meantime, here's what you need to know about our shipping charges:

• Orders under ₹499 carry a flat shipping fee of ₹49.

• Orders of ₹499 or more qualify for free shipping.

✦ A Small Note About Delays

We work with trusted delivery partners, but once your parcel leaves us, certain delays may be outside our hands.

Weather, public holidays, courier delays, incorrect addresses, or other unforeseen circumstances may occasionally add a little time to your delivery.

✦ Your Parcel Arrived Damaged?

Please don't worry. If your package arrives damaged, contact us as soon as possible with clear photographs/videos of the package and the item.

We'll look into it and guide you through the next steps.

✦ Need Help?

If your parcel seems to be taking longer than expected or you have any questions about your order, reach out to us through our official contact details.

Refund and Exchange Policy

At ORELLE By Alayna

We carefully inspect every order before dispatch to ensure it reaches you in perfect condition. Please read our policy carefully before placing your order.

✦ No Returns

We do not accept returns , once an order has been delivered. Please check the product details, size, colour, and other information carefully before placing your order.

✦ Damaged or Defective Products

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, please contact us within 24 HOURS of delivery.

To request an exchange, you must:

• Record a continuous, unedited/untrimmed unboxing video clearly showing the package from the moment it is opened.

• Share the video with us via WhatsApp within 24 HOURS of delivery.

•Keep the original packaging, tags, and product safely with you until the issue is resolved.

✦ Exchange Policy

If the product is confirmed to be damaged, defective, or incorrectly sent, we will review the issue and, if approved, provide an exchange as per product availability.

•Exchanges are not accepted for change of mind, incorrect choice, or personal preference

.

Important

Once an order is placed, please make sure you have checked all product details carefully. No returns are accepted, so please choose your products carefully before ordering.

Privacy Policy

At ORELLE by Alayna, your privacy matters to us. This page outlines what details we gather when you shop with us, how they're put to use, and the steps we take to keep them protected.

What we collect

When you place an order, we ask for your name, phone number, email, delivery address, and a landmark if you provide one. We never see or store your card, UPI, or banking details — all payments run through Razorpay, our payment partner, so that information stays entirely outside our systems.

Why we collect it

Your details are used purely to fulfil and deliver your order, reach out if there's an update on it, and reply to any messages or feedback you send us. We will never sell, rent, or hand over your personal information to third parties for their own use.

Who has access to it

We pass on your shipping information to our courier partners so your order can reach you, and your payment info is processed directly through Razorpay under their own security standards. Beyond that, nobody else sees your details.

How we keep it safe

We store your order and contact information securely, using it only for the reasons above. While we take sensible precautions to safeguard your data, no system connected to the internet can be called completely risk-free.

Your Rights

You may request to:

• Access the data you've shared with us

• Correct or delete your information

For any questions about how your information is handled, reach out via ContactUs.
